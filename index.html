May and Leaf- Handcrafted Bubble Tea

Rooted in tradition, crafted with care.

Mây & Leaf was born from a simple belief: that the best drinks are made with time, patience, and honest ingredients. Inspired by the softness of clouds (Mây) and the purity of fresh tea leaves (Leaf), we bring traditional tea craftsmanship and warmth to the streets of Hamburg. In our kitchen, there are no shortcuts, artificial powders, or syrups. Every morning, we brew high-mountain whole-leaf teas, crush real seasonal fruits, and gently steam our taro. Our tapioca pearls and chewy toppings are rolled and cooked by hand in small batches throughout the day. Topped with our signature cloud foam—a velvety layer reminiscent of gentle floating clouds—every cup is an invitation to slow down, take a breath, and savor a moment of pure comfort.
