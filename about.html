About Us

It all started on a typical grey, rain-dusted morning in Hamburg. Walking past the bustling coffee shops and fast-paced streets, there was a quiet longing for something different—a memory of home, where time moved at the speed of steeping tea.

Growing up, tea was never just a drink; it was a ritual of care. It was the sound of boiling kettles, the earthy aroma of whole leaves uncurling in hot water, and the patient hands of family steaming fresh taro and rolling tapioca pearls in small wooden kitchens. It was warmth, nostalgia, and pure simplicity.

Moving to Hamburg, we watched bubble tea become fast, colorful, and industrial—filled with artificial powders, bright neon syrups, and rushed preparation. We missed the soul of real tea.

That is why Mây & Leaf was was born.

We named our shop after two elements of pure nature: Leaf, representing high-mountain tea leaves harvested with reverence, and Mây—the Vietnamese word for cloud—symbolizing the soft, weightless sea-salt foam that crowns our cups.

At Mây & Leaf, we chose the slow path. In our open kitchen, there are no shortcuts. Every morning, we hand-pound seasonal fruits, gently steam fresh taro root, and simmer our tapioca pearls in raw brown sugar every three hours.

We created Mây & Leaf to be a quiet sanctuary in the heart of Hamburg—a place where traditional tea craft meets modern mindfulness. When you step inside, we invite you to shake off the cold, pause the rush of the city, and hold a small, warm cloud of comfort in your hands.
